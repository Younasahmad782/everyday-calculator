What this site does with your data

Everyday Calculator's tools run in your browser. Numbers you type into the calculator, unit converter, calendar, or other calculators are not sent to a server — they stay on your device and are only saved to your own browser's local storage (for example, your dark mode preference and recent calculation history), which you can clear at any time from this site's storage settings in your browser.

Currency converter

The currency converter requests current exchange rates from a third-party exchange-rate provider so it can show up-to-date conversions. This request does not include the amount you are converting.
