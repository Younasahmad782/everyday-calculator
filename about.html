About

Everyday Calculator is a free, simple set of tools for calculations most people run into day to day: a basic calculator, unit conversions, currency conversion, and a few date and percentage tools.

It's built to be fast and to work without an account. Calculations happen in your browser — nothing you type is sent anywhere except the currency page, which needs to fetch current exchange rates from a public rate provider.

If something isn't working, or a unit or currency you need is missing, get in touch through the contact page.
